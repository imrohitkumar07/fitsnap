# FitSnap Nutrition Data Sources, Licences & Attribution

FitSnap synthesizes authoritative, open-access, and public-domain nutritional databases into a single verified repository. No external LLMs or third-party AI models are used for nutrition data.

## 1. USDA FoodData Central (FNDDS, Foundation Foods, SR Legacy)
* **Provider:** Agricultural Research Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA)
* **Datasets Included:**
  * *Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS) 2021–2023 (Oct 2024)*: Prepared foods and multi-ingredient mixed dishes 'as eaten'.
  * *Foundation Foods (Oct 2024)*: Detailed nutrient profiles with measurement variability for core commodities.
  * *Standard Reference (SR) Legacy (Apr 2018)*: Historical standard reference data for single ingredients and basic foods.
* **Licence:** Public Domain (U.S. Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105). Free for unrestricted commercial and personal use.
* **Citation:** U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, 2024. `fdc.nal.usda.gov`.

## 2. Indian Food Composition Tables (ICMR / IFCT 2017)
* **Provider:** National Institute of Nutrition (NIN), Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), Hyderabad, India.
* **Coverage:** 30+ Certified staple Indian dishes, flatbreads (Paratha, Chapati, Naan), dals, sweets, and street food cooked under traditional preparation methods.
* **Citation:** Longvah, T., Ananthan, R., Bhaskarachary, K., & Venkaiah, K. (2017). *Indian Food Composition Tables*. National Institute of Nutrition, ICMR.

## 3. Open Food Facts (Packaged Goods Barcode Lookup)
* **Provider:** Open Food Facts contributors (`openfoodfacts.org`).
* **Licence:** Open Database Licence (ODbL) v1.0. Individual database contents licensed under Database Contents License (DbCL) v1.0.
* **Attribution:** Data retrieved via Open Food Facts API, locally cached in SQLite for offline privacy.

## 4. User Custom Recipes & Corrected Prototypes
* **Storage:** 100% Local SQLite database on device. Never transmitted or synced to external cloud services.
